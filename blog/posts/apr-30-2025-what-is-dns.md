### What is DNS?

DNS, or Domain Name System, is basically the phonebook of the internet. When you go over to your favorite website, quantumdev.cc, the Internet Protocol translates quantumdev.cc into an IP adress. In quantumdev.cc's case, it translates to 

```HTML 
104.21.112.1.
```
