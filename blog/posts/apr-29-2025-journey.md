# My Development Journey
## April 29, 2025

Hello everybody! Welcome to my first blog post! In this post I'm going to be telling you about my development journey.

My first experience with tech was probably in 2nd grade, when the whole world was in pandemic. My school had switched to online learning, as most schools had. I was issued a Lenovo Chromebook( Gen 2, I think, I have a Gen 4 now. ) I learned how to use a computer for the first time.

Because no one saw each other in real life, no one understood the expertise I had developed.

Until 3rd Grade.

I moved schools after 2nd grade, so I was new to my 3rd grade class. During 3rd grade, the pandemic was lifted, but we had to wear masks still. The current tech expert in the class was this boy named Siri and he was pretty good at tech. But then he moved. So everbody saw me as the tech expert! 

I understood that I had a good learning of tech at the time, I didn't even know it until now, but I could type faster then most kids, and even most teachers! I now have a WPM of 135, so I can type faster then you can speak. 

Once I got to fifth grade, I knew more than all people in my class, including the teacher. This is when I started my journey in Roblox Studio, with my best friend, Noah(Not his real name.) You can check out his website at [noah.quantumdev.cc](https://noah.quantumdev.cc) < CLICK. 

Fun fact: To this day, I still don't know Luau(Roblox scripting). But I know my fair share of UI.

I started my web development journey in 5th grade too. I made a simple website with just HTML for my lawn service business(didn't work out, Noah helped too.)

And that was when I realized, I wanted to do web development. For a while, I just learned from YouTube, but it was way too disorganized for me, and I ended up getting a Udemy course, which I am learning right now!

And that is basically my journey! Not really finished, since I can do WAYYYY more things in the future.

So hopefully, I will become an awesome dev!
